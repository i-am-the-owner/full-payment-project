What the market looks like

Most transfer apps share the same parts: a coloured brand header, a converter with two boxes, and a list of transactions. They differ mainly in colour. Our previous design used dark teal and mint, which sits too close to Wise.

WiseForest green with electric lime, heavy display type, pill buttons.
Western UnionYellow and black, the classic remittance look.
RemitlyNavy and bright blue, the most common fintech colour.
RevolutBlack and white with gradient cards.
MonzoHot coral on navy, friendly and rounded.
Our earlier draftDark teal and mint. Read as a Wise lookalike.

Three ideas, used everywhere

These replace the usual converter-and-list layout. Each one answers a real question customers have when sending money home.

The ticket

Every transfer is a ticket from one currency and city to another, with a tear-off stub for the amount. It doubles as the receipt and gets stamped on delivery.

  1. Top: the route. FROM code, a dotted line, TO code, cities underneath. Filled with the status colour.
  2. Tear line with two notches. Always in the same place, between route and amount.
  3. Stub: the amount. White, dark blue text, amount left-aligned and biggest on screen. The status pill sits on the right of the stub.
  4. Swap button. Dark blue circle between the blue and green tickets, only where the customer types an amount.
  5. Two sizes only. Large (full width, route 58pt) for one transfer; small (52 high top) for lists. Both use the full screen width minus 22pt each side, stacked straight with 10pt gaps. Never rotated.
  6. One large ticket per screen. Lists use small tickets only.
Always
  • App colours: blue, dark blue and white. No black.
  • Buttons dark blue (white on dark screens); links and the selected tab blue
  • Ticket colour matches the step, with the step also written in words
  • Backgrounds white or light grey
Never
  • Amber, green or red on buttons or backgrounds
  • A ticket colour that does not match its step
  • Tickets at an angle or overlapping
  • A country picker on log in or sign up (UK only)
